What is beech plywood?

Beech plywood is built from peeled beech veneers — at our mill, roughly 2.6 mm per ply — laid with the grain alternating long and cross, then bonded under heat and pressure. The result is a panel that behaves like engineered hardwood: dimensionally stable in both directions, heavy, stiff, and hard right through the face.

Cildro panels are 100% beech in every single ply. There are no poplar or softwood filler cores hiding inside the sandwich — the property sheet you read below applies to the whole panel, not just its face veneer.

That single-species core is what separates true beech plywood from “beech-faced” panels sold in DIY stores, which carry a beech look on a lighter, softer core and share none of beech’s screw-holding or dent resistance.

Why beech instead of birch?

Baltic birch earned its reputation honestly — but European beech is simply the denser, harder species, and since the EU restricted Russian and Belarusian birch, it is also the panel that is actually available, in stock, with clean EU paperwork.

What beech plywood is used for

Because the hardness runs through every ply, beech plywood is specified where panels take real abuse:

  • Furniture & cabinetry — exposed edges polish like solid wood; screws bite and stay put.
  • Worktops — ≥25 N/mm² Brinell surface shrugs off daily impact.
  • Shopfitting & interiors — uniform faces under clear finish, tight CNC detail.
  • Structural decking — 75 N/mm² bending strength, engineered consistency.
  • Heavy-duty packaging — survives forklifts and export handling.
  • Trailer floors & vehicle bodies — as film-faced Fagotex.

Our panels run in supply chains for furniture, trailer and vehicle manufacturers across the EU and UK — including Fliegl Trailer, James Latham, Holver, Slovenijales and Broszeit.

Beech plywood FAQ

What is beech plywood?

A hardwood panel pressed from European beech (Fagus sylvatica) veneers. Cildro beech plywood is 100% beech core — no filler species — 750 kg/m³ dense, ≥25 N/mm² Brinell hard, available 4–60 mm thick.

How does beech plywood compare to birch plywood?

Beech plywood is denser (750 vs ~650 kg/m³) and harder than Baltic birch, so it holds screws tighter and dents less. Anywhere birch worked, beech works at least as well — full comparison here.
